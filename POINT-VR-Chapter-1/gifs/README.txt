This folder contains animations of the different possible polarizations of gravitational waves. While gravitational waves in general relativity have only two polarizations, some modified theories of gravity can have up to six. The polarizations are called Plus, Cross, Breathing, Longitudinal, X, and Y because of the pattern with which they stretch and squeeze the spacetime they pass through. Here, those patterns are animated as a gravitational wave passes through a tube of test particles along the direction of the red arrow. The files are labeled with the name of the polarization and the angle that the tube is viewed from. For example, Breathing_45.gif illustrates the breathing mode, propagating down the tube as viewed from a 45° angle. Files labeled with multiple polarization names are showing those polarization together. For example, PlusCross_45 shows both the plus and cross modes, which are active in general relativity. The files labeled all show all six polarization at once.
